Key benefits

  • Supports high, persistent egg production
  • Strong shells, fewer cracks
  • Keeps hens in good condition

Directions for use

  1. Feed as the only ration from point of lay (about 5% production) onwards.
  2. Not suitable for young pullets: the high calcium level is meant for laying hens only.
  3. Provide clean, fresh drinking water at all times.
  4. Change from the previous feed gradually over 3 to 5 days.
  5. Do not feed feed that is mouldy, wet, caked or smells musty.
